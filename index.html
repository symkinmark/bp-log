Before you measure
  • Sit quietly for about 5 minutes first.
  • Back supported, feet flat on the floor, legs uncrossed.
  • Cuff on bare skin, arm resting at heart level.
  • No talking or moving during the reading.
  • Take 3 readings about 1 minute apart, unless your doctor said otherwise.
  • Morning: fasting, before breakfast. Afternoon: 2 hours after lunch. Evening: before dinner.
  • Use the same arm all week.

Your log stays on this device

Everything you enter is saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere: no account, no server, no analytics. That also means the log won’t show up on another device, and clearing browser data or removing the app deletes it. Keep a backup.

Back up and restore

A backup file holds every week. Restoring one adds its weeks to what’s already here.
